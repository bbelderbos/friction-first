## Definition of Done (for the 30 sessions)

- 30 friction-log entries committed in `logs/`.
- Every project shipped (working, runnable, README'd) — whether that's one escalating project or three.
- A short retrospective (`retro.md`) covering: what surprised you, where you felt the most growth, where AI hurt vs helped, what you'd change about the protocol.

The retro is the most reusable artifact. Future-you and any audience (post, podcast, talk) gets more from the retro than from the code.
