# Design Intent — Session <N>

> Write this in the first 5 minutes of your session, *before* opening the editor. Before opening the tutor. Before reading yesterday's code.
>
> If you cannot articulate what you're about to build, you are not ready to build it. The act of writing this is the work.

## What I'm building today

One sentence. The smallest meaningful slice of the project I can finish in this session.

## How I'll approach it

Two or three sentences. What data structures, what functions, what shape the solution takes. Be specific enough that a future-you reading this could predict the diff.

## What I expect to be hard

One or two sentences. Where do I expect to get stuck? Borrow checker? An async pattern I haven't used? A library API I'm not familiar with?

Naming the expected friction in advance is half the value. It primes you to engage when you hit it, instead of pattern-matching to a known answer or asking the tutor to dissolve it.

## What I will *not* do today

One sentence. The scope creep I'm choosing to defer. Today is for X, not Y.

## How I'll know I'm done

One sentence. Concrete acceptance criterion. "Tests pass" is not enough. "Tests pass and the function handles empty input correctly" is enough.

---

When this is filled in, save it (optionally append to a `design-intents/` folder), then open the editor. Not before.
